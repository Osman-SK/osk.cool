DefiLlama Search

March 15, 2026

One of the most underrated web3 product for onchain natives is probably DefiLlama Search [1]. Let me explain.

Onchain natives have a common problem:

The fear of going to a fake/scam website that may empty their crypto wallets.

How many times have we seen on social media of a person who was hacked, phished, clicked on the wrong link, did not even understand how he/she was hacked?

If you are onchain native, chances are you have seen these personal stories way too many times.

Enter Defi Llama Search [1]. Defi Llama [2] originally became famous for its open-source TVL tracking metrics and dashboards but later greatly expanded its data offerings. Now it has so many products I am sure I do not know many.

Though I do know one: Defi Llama Search.

It is just a simple web search interface that serves a very important function: bypassing fake links on google, X, and any other websites. It gets you safely to the web3 product.

How many millions would have stayed in victim wallets had they knew and used Defi Llama Search? Lots of millions as onchain sleuth ZachXBT exposed many sinister institutional business models (scam links on google, CoinBase security vulnerabilities etc.) [3]. Unless it hits big tech wallets, they will surely sell you out.

So watch out. Do not be news on Rekt News [4]. Defi Llama is as good as it gets to be an OG crypto firm with onchain natives in mind.
